# Método de las ilustraciones transparentes

Se usó el editor de imágenes integrado con transparencia real y la captura adjunta como referencia. Los recortes en originales/ se obtuvieron directamente de la captura.

## Escritorio
Use case: background-extraction. Extract only the complete right-hand programmer illustration: person seen from behind at desk, two monitors, night city window, plants, lamp, shelves and chair. Remove all website text, header, buttons, owl and lower landscape. Preserve composition, colors and pixel-art details as closely as possible. Transparent exterior, retain dark interior details, full uncropped subject.

## Búho
Use case: background-extraction. Isolate only the purple owl at bottom left. Preserve golden eyes, orange beak and feet, cyan accents, original pose and pixel-art proportions as closely as possible. Remove rocks, foliage, trail, stars and all interface text. True transparent background, full owl.

## Paisaje inferior
Use case: background-extraction. Extract only the panoramic lower strip: purple rocks, tiny fir trees, cyan plants, distant city and forest, and winding dashed pink/cyan/white trail ending in a ring and cyan arrow. Remove owl, workstation, text and buttons. Preserve the wide layout and original palette as closely as possible. Transparent background around the landscape.

Los SVG se dibujaron como geometría vectorial; no proceden de vectorizar automáticamente la imagen ni de incrustar PNG.
